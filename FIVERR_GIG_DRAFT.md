```text
I manually move a small batch of your active eBay listings to Etsy and send you a spreadsheet of your item-specifics/template data to keep. One-time job: no software, no bots, no subscription.

WHAT YOU WILL RECEIVE

WHAT I NEED FROM YOU
- Your eBay Seller Hub "All active listings" report and listing links
- Which listings to move

- Your Etsy price and shipping choices

NOT INCLUDED
More listings than your package, other marketplaces, ongoing sync, photography/editing, Etsy shop setup/SEO/ads, sales guarantees. Etsy's fees are yours. Items must be allowed under Etsy's seller policies.

I only use the information in your listings; I don't write new listing content.
```

## 5. Buyer requirements (Fiverr "Requirements" questions)

Shown to the buyer after ordering. Fiverr auto-cancels an order if requirements are not submitted within 14 days (`VALIDATION_GATE.md` §24.2), so the first line is worded to prompt a quick reply.

**Intro / instruction text (if the editor offers one):**

```text
Please send the details below so I can start. NEVER send me any password (eBay, Etsy, or email). I don't need one.
```

**Question 1 — Attachment (required)**

```text
Upload your eBay listing data. In eBay Seller Hub: Reports tab > Download > Listings > All active listings > download the CSV or XLS file. If you can't use Seller Hub, upload a CSV or screenshots of the listings instead.
```

**Question 2 — Free text (required)**

```text
Which listings should I move? Paste the eBay item numbers or links for the listings, up to the number in your package.
```

**Question 3 — Multiple choice (required)**

```text
Do you already have an Etsy shop? (Yes / No)
```

**Question 4 — Multiple choice (required)**

```text
How should the Etsy side be done? (A) Etsy Shared Access: I'll invite you as a team member, you sign in with your own account, nothing else needed. (B) I'll publish the listings myself from your ready-to-copy sheet.
```

**Question 5 — Free text (required)**

```text
Etsy price and shipping: should I use the same prices as on eBay, or do you want different ones? Which Etsy shipping profile (or shipping details) should the listings use?
```

**Question 6 — Free text (optional)**

```text
Anything Etsy-specific you want included that eBay doesn't have (for example tags or a shop section)? Send the exact values. I won't invent them.
```

**Question 7 — Multiple choice (required)**

```text
Please confirm: I own these listings and photos, and my items are allowed under Etsy's seller policies. (I confirm)
```

Note on Shared Access (used in the order chat after answer 4 = A; not a form field): Etsy Shared Access invitations expire after 7 days (`VALIDATION_GATE.md` §23.3, Etsy primary source), so the buyer should be asked to send the invite promptly.

## 6. FAQ

Each answer is ≤300 characters. Select the ones you want; the order below is recommended, most trust-critical first.

**Q: Do you need my eBay or Etsy password?**

```text
No. I never ask for, receive or store any eBay or Etsy password. You keep full ownership and control of both accounts the whole time.
```

**Q: How do you publish on Etsy without my password?**

```text
Through Etsy's own Shared Access feature. You invite me as a team member; I sign in with my own Etsy account, can create and publish listings, and cannot see finances or settings. You can remove my access at any time.
```

**Q: What if I don't want to use Shared Access?**

```text
Then I prepare a ready-to-copy sheet with the finished title, description, price, category and item-specifics text for each listing, and you publish them yourself in your own Etsy account. Etsy has no bulk upload for new listings, so this is done listing by listing.
```

**Q: Is this automated?**

```text
No. It's done by hand, by me. No bots, no third-party automation tools, and no AI-generated listing content beyond the information you supply.
```

**Q: Will you rewrite or improve my listings?**

```text
No. I use your existing titles, descriptions, photos and item-specifics as supplied. Etsy-only fields with no eBay equivalent are filled only with values you send me.
```

**Q: Is this a subscription? Will it keep syncing?**

```text
No. It's a one-time job for the batch in your package. No ongoing sync, relisting or maintenance after delivery, and no other marketplaces. eBay to Etsy only.
```

**Q: What if I have more listings than my package covers?**

```text
Only the number of listings in your package is included. Choose the package that fits your batch. If your data turns out to need clearly more work than the package covers, I'll tell you before I start.
```

**Q: Do you guarantee sales?**

```text
No. I cannot promise views, sales or rankings. I deliver the defined batch of listings and the spreadsheet. Etsy's own fees are paid by you to Etsy.
```

Character counts of every FAQ answer are listed in section 11.
