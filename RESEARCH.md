# Research

Use this file for current evidence only.

## Rules

Each candidate gets a compact record.

### Candidate template

## [Candidate name]

**Status:** DISCOVERY / VALIDATION / KEEP / KILL / BLOCKED / INCONCLUSIVE

**Problem:**  
What concrete operational problem exists?

**Buyer:**  
Who experiences it and who can authorize payment?

**Existing solution:**  
What do they use today?

**Existing spending/effort:**  
What do they already pay, or what recurring effort/workaround do they spend?

**Dissatisfaction:**  
What observable evidence shows that the current solution is insufficient?

**Wedge:**  
What is the smallest useful product that could solve an important part of the problem?

**Distribution:**  
How can a problem-aware user discover this product without cold outreach?

**Payment evidence:**  
What evidence suggests that the buyer may actually pay?

**Feasibility:**  
Can one developer build and operate the first version?

**Support / platform risk:**  
What could make this expensive or difficult to operate?

**Validation experiment:**  
What is the cheapest test that can answer the next commercial question?

**Evidence:**
- source:
- observation:
- date:
- interpretation:

**Decision:**
One paragraph. No artificial score.

## Research discipline

Do not create a large report for a candidate before answering the commercial gates.

Use citations/links for external evidence.

Prefer primary sources and direct customer behavior over summaries and opinion pieces.

Record important negative evidence as well as positive evidence.

Do not reopen KILL decisions without materially new evidence.
