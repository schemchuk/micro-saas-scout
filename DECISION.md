# Decision Log

The purpose of this file is to prevent circular research.

## Decision states

### KEEP
Evidence is strong enough to continue.

### TEST
The hypothesis is sufficiently promising to run a cheap commercial validation.

### KILL
A critical assumption failed or the wedge is clearly unavailable/overserved.

### BLOCKED
The opportunity may be interesting, but a missing dependency prevents valid testing.

### INCONCLUSIVE
Evidence is insufficient after a reasonable test. Do not automatically respond by launching another endless research phase.

## Decision entry template

## [YYYY-MM-DD] — [Candidate]

**Decision:** KEEP / TEST / KILL / BLOCKED / INCONCLUSIVE

**Question being decided:**  
What specific uncertainty were we trying to resolve?

**Evidence:**  
Only the evidence relevant to that decision.

**Reasoning:**  
Separate observed facts from inference.

**Next action:**  
One concrete next step, or "Stop".

## Global stop rule

If research produces repeated evidence that no candidate satisfies:

`pain + buyer + money + dissatisfaction + wedge + distribution`

do not simply add more searches.

Change one strategic assumption and start a new experiment.

## Project-level success condition

The project is successful when it identifies a candidate that justifies a cheap real-world payment/discovery test.

It is not successful merely because it produces:

- a large list of niches;
- many competitor profiles;
- many research pages;
- a high theoretical score.
