# Micro-SaaS Scout — Context

## Purpose

Find and validate a micro-SaaS opportunity that a solo developer can realistically launch and sell.

The project is not primarily about finding a "niche". It is about finding a small, concrete business problem where:

1. people already spend time or money dealing with the problem;
2. existing solutions are unsatisfactory, fragmented, expensive, or operationally painful;
3. a narrow product can solve the problem materially better;
4. there is a realistic path to discovering the product without cold outreach;
5. the first paying customers can be reached before a large implementation effort.

## Operating assumptions

- Solo developer.
- Very low initial budget; target is approximately €0 for research and near-zero for the first validation.
- No cold outreach as a default acquisition method.
- Inbound acquisition is allowed: search, communities, marketplaces, directories, educational content, public demos, Product Hunt-style launches, social content, and other channels where customers can discover the product without one-to-one cold selling.
- A small amount of direct customer interaction is allowed for validation, support, interviews, and feedback. This does not mean building a sales-heavy business.
- The product should be technically feasible for one developer.
- Avoid business models that require large teams, heavy operations, regulated infrastructure, or enterprise procurement unless the evidence is unusually strong.
- Do not build an MVP merely to make the research feel productive.

## Core question

Not:

> "What software niche has low competition?"

But:

> "Where is there a repeated, monetized problem with visible dissatisfaction and a credible path from discovery to first payment?"
